Observed while running make race:
TestOperatorNetworkCeilingAndRebinding: DNS rebinding escaped network ceiling

Investigation: the fixture changed its resolver after calling CloseIdleConnections,
but an already approved pooled TCP connection could still be reused. Reusing that
connection cannot dial the newly supplied address. The assertion tested forced
DNS resolution, rather than the intended rule that every new dial checks the IP.

Repair: disable keep-alives in this fixture and fully consume its first response,
then change the resolver. Production keeps connection reuse; no destination rule
is disabled. Replay must prove the next dial refuses the unauthorized address and
that the origin saw only the initial approved request. This was a fixture defect,
not evidence of an unauthorized production connection.
